CATEGORY: AIRCRAFT BASICS
==================================================


Q: What is an aircraft?

A: An aircraft is a machine designed to fly through the atmosphere using aerodynamic forces. Aircraft include airplanes, helicopters, gliders, and other flying vehicles.


Q: What are the main parts of an aircraft?

A: The main parts of an aircraft include the fuselage, wings, empennage (tail section), landing gear, engines, flight control surfaces, and avionics systems.


Q: What is the purpose of wings on an aircraft?

A: Wings generate lift that allows an aircraft to fly. They are designed to create aerodynamic forces when air moves over their surfaces.


Q: What is the fuselage?

A: The fuselage is the main body structure of an aircraft. It houses passengers, cargo, cockpit systems, and other equipment.


Q: What is the cockpit?

A: The cockpit is the area where pilots operate and monitor the aircraft using flight controls, displays, and communication systems.



==================================================
CATEGORY: AIRCRAFT SYSTEMS
==================================================


Q: What are aircraft systems?

A: Aircraft systems are integrated components that support aircraft operation, including electrical, hydraulic, fuel, environmental control, navigation, and flight control systems.


Q: What is the hydraulic system used for?

A: Hydraulic systems provide controlled power for aircraft functions such as flight controls, landing gear operation, and braking systems.


Q: What is the electrical system?

A: The aircraft electrical system supplies power to avionics, lighting, instruments, communication systems, and other aircraft equipment.


Q: What is avionics?

A: Avionics refers to electronic systems used in aircraft, including navigation, communication, monitoring, and flight management systems.


Q: What is the purpose of the fuel system?

A: The fuel system stores, manages, and supplies fuel to aircraft engines while maintaining safe operation.

CATEGORY: AIRCRAFT MAINTENANCE
==================================================


Q: What is aircraft maintenance?

A: Aircraft maintenance includes inspection, servicing, testing, repair, and replacement activities performed to maintain aircraft safety and airworthiness.


Q: Why is aircraft maintenance important?

A: Maintenance ensures aircraft reliability, safety, regulatory compliance, and helps prevent failures.


Q: What are maintenance checks?

A: Maintenance checks are scheduled inspections performed at defined intervals to assess aircraft condition and ensure continued safe operation.


Q: What is preventive maintenance?

A: Preventive maintenance consists of planned actions performed to reduce the possibility of equipment failure.


Q: What is corrective maintenance?

A: Corrective maintenance addresses identified faults or defects to restore a system to an acceptable operating condition.


Q: What is a maintenance log?

A: A maintenance log is an official record of inspections, findings, actions performed, and aircraft technical status.

CATEGORY: AVIATION SAFETY
==================================================


Q: What is aviation safety?

A: Aviation safety involves practices, procedures, regulations, and systems designed to reduce risks during aircraft operations.


Q: What is a safety management system (SMS)?

A: A Safety Management System is a structured approach used by aviation organizations to identify hazards, manage risks, and improve safety performance.


Q: Why are pre-flight inspections important?

A: Pre-flight inspections help identify visible issues and confirm aircraft readiness before operation.


Q: What is airworthiness?

A: Airworthiness means an aircraft meets approved safety standards and is considered suitable for safe operation.


Q: What is a hazard?

A: A hazard is a condition or factor that could potentially cause harm, damage, or operational risk.



==================================================
CATEGORY: FLIGHT OPERATIONS
==================================================


Q: What is a flight plan?

A: A flight plan contains information about a planned flight, including route, timing, aircraft details, and operational information.


Q: What is turbulence?

A: Turbulence is irregular air movement that can cause changes in aircraft motion during flight.


Q: Why are seat belts required during flight?

A: Seat belts help protect occupants from sudden aircraft movements and unexpected turbulence.


Q: What is a cockpit warning?

A: A cockpit warning is an alert generated by aircraft systems to inform pilots about a condition requiring attention.



==================================================
CATEGORY: ENGINES
==================================================


Q: What are the main types of aircraft engines?

A: Common aircraft engines include piston engines, turboprop engines, turbofan engines, and turbojet engines.


Q: What is a turbofan engine?

A: A turbofan engine is a jet engine commonly used in commercial aviation that produces thrust using a combination of core engine operation and fan airflow.


Q: Why are engine inspections important?

A: Engine inspections help detect wear, damage, or abnormal conditions and support safe aircraft operation.



==================================================
CATEGORY: EMERGENCY AND SAFETY RESPONSE
==================================================


Q: What should passengers do during an aircraft emergency?

A: Passengers should remain calm, follow crew instructions, use safety equipment as directed, and comply with emergency procedures.


Q: Why are emergency procedures important?

A: Emergency procedures provide standardized responses to abnormal situations and improve safety outcomes.


Q: Who is responsible for aircraft safety?

A: Aircraft safety is supported by pilots, maintenance personnel, engineers, air traffic services, operators, regulators, and passengers following procedures.



==================================================
CATEGORY: REGULATIONS
==================================================


Q: What are aviation regulations?

A: Aviation regulations are rules established by aviation authorities to ensure safe aircraft operation and maintenance.


Q: What is an aviation authority?

A: An aviation authority is an organization responsible for regulating and overseeing aviation safety within a region or country.


Q: Why is documentation important in aviation?

A: Documentation provides traceability, accountability, maintenance history, and supports safe aviation operations.
